# Superstore Europe Data Analysis & Tableau Dashboard

Interactive Tableau dashboard and business intelligence analysis for a European retail superstore.  
This project focuses on sales monitoring, profit analysis, shipping performance, and marketing budget allocation using a data-driven approach.

---

## Project Overview

The goal of this project was to support business decision-making by transforming raw operational data into interactive dashboards and strategic insights.

The analysis was developed using Tableau and includes:

- Sales trend monitoring
- Profit analysis across European countries
- Shipping performance analysis
- Marketing budget allocation recommendations
- Interactive filtering and exploratory analysis

---

## Business Objectives

The project addresses two main business needs:

### 1. Operational Monitoring Dashboard
An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to monitor key business metrics, including:

- Sales trends over time
- Profit by country
- Quantity of shipped products by shipping class
- Product and category performance

### 2. Marketing Strategy Storytelling
A Tableau Story designed to support marketing budget allocation decisions by identifying:

- High-potential products deserving increased investment
- Saturated products with limited growth opportunities
- Low-performing products potentially suitable for removal

## Key Insights

Some important findings from the analysis include:

- Certain European countries generated high sales volumes but lower profit margins.
- Some product sub-categories showed strong profitability despite relatively low sales volume, indicating growth potential.
- Several product groups appeared saturated, suggesting reduced marketing efficiency.
- Shipping class selection had a measurable impact on operational performance and logistics efficiency.

---

## Analytical Choice: Sub-Category vs Product-Level Analysis

Initial product-level visualizations produced highly fragmented and difficult-to-interpret charts due to the large number of individual products.

To improve readability and support business decision-making, the analysis was aggregated at the **sub-category level**, while preserving the ability to inspect individual products through Tableau’s interactive exploration features.

This approach improved:
- interpretability
- strategic comparison
- dashboard usability
- storytelling effectiveness
